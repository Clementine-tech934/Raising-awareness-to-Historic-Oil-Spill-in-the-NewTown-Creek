Introduction

Newtown Creek is a place that used to be a healthy piece of water, but consequential and careless oil spillage through soil absorption has caused the creek to become dirty and mostly dangerous for marine life living there. Newtown Creek is part of the Hudson Estuary, flowing west for 3.8 miles between Queens and Brooklyn and emptying into the East River. The Creek comprises small branches known as: Dutch Kills, Maspeth Creek, Whale Creek, the East Branch, and English Kills. It is a tidally influenced estuary with a total surface area of 140 acres. Its natural depth was 12 feet, but today it can be as shallow as four feet.

Importance

Decades of poor maintenance and leaks from many oil storage facilities and refineries caused a buildup of oil, leading to the Greenpoint Sewer explosion, and the Oil spill in NewTown Creek. The purpose of this website is to help raise awareness about the oil spill, and inform people how it negatively affects the environment of NewTown Creek.
